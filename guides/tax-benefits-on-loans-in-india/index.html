Tax Benefits on Loans in India: Home, Education and Business

A general overview of tax deductions linked to home loans, education loans and business loans in India, with a reminder to check current law.

By the NEXUS Lending Partners team · Last updated 13 November 2026

Tax rules change with each budget and depend on the tax regime you choose. Use this as a general overview and confirm with a chartered accountant.

Home loan

Under the old tax regime, interest on a home loan for a self-occupied property and principal repayment may be deductible within statutory limits under specific sections. Such deductions are generally not available under the new regime, except for certain let-out property cases.

Education loan

Interest paid on an education loan for higher studies may be deductible for up to a specified number of years under the relevant section, subject to the law in force.

Business loan

Interest on a loan taken for business is usually allowed as a business expense, subject to the Income Tax Act and proper accounting.

Personal loan

Typically no deduction, unless the funds are used for a deductible purpose such as business or house purchase, and the documentation supports it.

This is general information, not tax advice.

Note: This guide is general information, not financial, legal or tax advice. Rules and rates change. Confirm with the lender or a qualified professional.
