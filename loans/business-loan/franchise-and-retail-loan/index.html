Eligibility

  • Business vintage typically 1 to 3 years minimum
  • Annual turnover meeting lender thresholds, commonly ₹10 lakh to ₹40 lakh+
  • ITR and GST filings up to date
  • Credit score 700+ for the owner; business credit history helps
  • Business premises proof and registration (Udyam, GST, shop licence)

Documents required

  • PAN, Aadhaar of owners and business PAN
  • Udyam registration, GST certificate, shop licence
  • ITR for 2 to 3 years with computation and financials
  • Bank statements for 6 to 12 months
  • GST returns for 12 months
  • Address proof of office or shop, and ownership or rent agreement

How the process works

  1. Tell us what you needShare the amount, purpose and basic details on WhatsApp or the form. We do not ask for ID numbers or OTPs at this stage.
  2. We check fit and optionsWe compare your profile with the typical criteria of partner lenders and suggest the loan type and lenders that suit you. The final eligibility decision is the lender's.
  3. Prepare your documentsYou get a precise checklist for your loan type, so you apply once with everything matching.
  4. Apply and verify with the lenderYou share GST, ITR, bank statements and financials. The lender may visit the premises and verify the business.
  5. Sanction and Key Fact StatementYou receive the sanction letter and the Key Fact Statement showing APR, EMI, fees and cooling-off terms. Read them fully before accepting.
  6. Disbursal and after-careThe lender releases funds directly to your bank account. We stay available for questions about repayment, prepayment and your next need.

Fees and charges

  • Processing fee: commonly 1% to 3% plus GST
  • Foreclosure charges of 2% to 5% on many loans, check the KFS
  • Credit guarantee fee where applicable under scheme-backed loans
  • Documentation charges on larger secured loans

Franchise and Retail Business Loan: frequently asked questions

Do franchises get better terms?
Often yes, when the brand is established.
What is the minimum business vintage for a loan?
Most lenders prefer at least 1 to 3 years of operation. Startups with strong revenue may be considered by some lenders or through scheme-backed products.
Can I get a business loan without collateral?
Yes, up to certain limits, often with credit-guarantee backing for MSMEs. Larger amounts usually need security.
Do I need a GST registration?
Many lenders require it if you are above the GST threshold. Some products accept bank-statement-based underwriting without GST.
