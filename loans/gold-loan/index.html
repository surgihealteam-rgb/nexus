What is a gold loan?

A gold loan is a secured loan where you pledge gold jewellery or ornaments with a lender. The loan amount depends on the gold's weight, purity and the lender's loan-to-value ratio. It is one of the fastest loans to get because income documentation is minimal.

Under RBI norms, lenders usually fund up to a stated percentage of the gold's value. NEXUS helps you compare partner lenders on interest rate, repayment options (monthly interest, bullet, EMI), valuation transparency and safekeeping. We recommend you visit the lender's branch for appraisal, since gold is physically assessed.

Who can apply

  • Small business owners needing working capital
  • Households with urgent cash needs
  • Farmers with seasonal needs
  • Borrowers with low credit scores who have gold

Features and benefits

  • Quick approval after physical appraisal
  • Minimal income documents; credit score matters less
  • Multiple repayment options: monthly interest, bullet, EMI
  • Gold is returned on full repayment
  • Part-release of gold on part-payment, with some lenders

Eligibility criteria

Each lender sets its own rules. These are typical conditions:

  • Age 18 or above (some lenders require 21+)
  • Gold ornaments of 18 to 24 carat purity, as accepted by the lender
  • Valid ID and address proof
  • Gold coins and bars are accepted by few lenders, with limits

Documents required

Keep these ready to avoid delays. Your lender may ask for more depending on your profile. Use our document checklist tool for a printable list.

  • PAN or Aadhaar, and address proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Gold ornaments (physical appraisal at branch)
  • Bank account details for disbursal

Fees and charges to look out for

  • Processing fee: typically 0.25% to 1% of loan, sometimes waived
  • Appraisal and storage charges as per lender
  • Overdue interest and auction notice charges if you default
  • Prepayment: many lenders allow it, some with a minimum interest period

Smart tips before you apply

  • Compare the effective interest rate, not the monthly rate shown on the poster
  • Ask for a written valuation: weight, purity and rate applied
  • Prefer lenders with insured, transparent storage
  • Track the maturity date; unpaid loans can lead to gold auction after notice

Frequently asked questions

How much gold loan can I get per gram?
It depends on that day's gold rate, purity and the lender's loan-to-value ratio. Lenders typically lend up to a regulated percentage of the gold's value.
Is my gold safe with the lender?
Reputed banks and NBFCs store gold in secured vaults and insure it. Ask for the lender's custody and insurance terms.
Can I get a gold loan with a low credit score?
Gold loans rely mainly on the collateral, so a low score is often not a barrier, but interest rates may reflect risk.
What happens if I cannot repay?
The lender sends notice and can auction the gold per its policy and law after the due date. Talk to the lender early about extending or renewing.
Which repayment option is best?
Monthly interest lets you repay principal later but is easy to roll. EMI loans reduce principal steadily. Choose based on cash flow.
