About loan against salary (salary advance)

A loan against salary is a short, small-ticket loan linked to your next salary credit, often with repayment on payday. It is expensive if used repeatedly.

Best suited for: Occasional gaps before month-end.

NEXUS tip: Use sparingly. Repeated use means a budget problem that a cheaper personal loan or savings buffer should fix.

Loan Against Salary (Salary Advance): key facts

These are typical figures for the wider category of personal loan. Your lender's offer may differ.

Typical amount₹50,000 to ₹40 lakh (varies by lender and profile)
Tenure12 to 84 months
Indicative rateRoughly 10.5% to 24% p.a. for most salaried borrowers; some higher-risk profiles are offered more. Always compare the APR in the Key Fact Statement.
SecurityUnsecured (no collateral)
Please note: Rates, amounts and terms on this page are indicative and vary by lender and your profile. NEXUS is a loan facilitator, not a lender. Approval and pricing are decided by the lender. Check the Key Fact Statement for the APR and all charges.

Eligibility

  • Age typically 21 to 60 years (some lenders accept up to 65 for self-employed)
  • Minimum net monthly income usually ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 depending on city and lender
  • Minimum 6 months to 1 year in current job (salaried) or 2+ years in business (self-employed)
  • Credit score generally 700 or above gives the widest choice; 750+ gets the best pricing
  • Total EMIs (existing plus new) generally kept within 40% to 50% of net monthly income

Documents required

  • PAN card and Aadhaar (KYC)
  • Latest 3 to 6 months salary slips (salaried)
  • Last 6 months bank statements showing salary credit
  • Form 16 or last 2 years ITR (self-employed, or if requested)
  • Business proof and GST registration, if self-employed
  • Current address proof and recent passport-size photograph

  • Late payment penalty and bounce charges as per your sanction letter
  • Always read the Key Fact Statement for APR and every fee before accepting

Loan Against Salary (Salary Advance): frequently asked questions

How is it different from a personal loan?
Tenure is typically 1 to 3 months, amounts are smaller, and effective annualised cost is higher.
How quickly can I get a personal loan?
Some digital lenders approve within hours when documents are ready and your profile is straightforward. Banks may take 2 to 7 working days. Timelines depend on the lender and verification, and we cannot guarantee a specific disbursal time.
Can I get a personal loan without salary slips?
Self-employed applicants and some salaried borrowers can use bank statements and ITR instead. The options narrow without income proof, so keep 6 to 12 months of bank statements handy.
Will applying hurt my credit score?
A formal application triggers a credit enquiry, which can cause a small, temporary dip. Multiple applications in a short window can add up, so shortlist first.
