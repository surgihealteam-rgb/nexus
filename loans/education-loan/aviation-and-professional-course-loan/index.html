Please note: Rates, amounts and terms on this page are indicative and vary by lender and your profile. NEXUS is a loan facilitator, not a lender. Approval and pricing are decided by the lender. Check the Key Fact Statement for the APR and all charges.

Eligibility

  • Indian citizen admitted to a recognised course
  • Co-applicant (parent or guardian) with stable income
  • Admission letter and fee structure
  • For larger loans, collateral or guarantor may be required

Documents required

  • Admission letter and fee structure
  • Academic records (10th, 12th, graduation, entrance scores)
  • KYC of student and co-applicant
  • Co-applicant income proof and bank statements
  • Collateral documents, if applicable
  • Visa and I-20 / CAS, for overseas courses

Use our document checklist to prepare everything before you apply.

How the process works

  1. Tell us what you needShare the amount, purpose and basic details on WhatsApp or the form. We do not ask for ID numbers or OTPs at this stage.
  2. We check fit and optionsWe compare your profile with the typical criteria of partner lenders and suggest the loan type and lenders that suit you. The final eligibility decision is the lender's.
  3. Prepare your documentsYou get a precise checklist for your loan type, so you apply once with everything matching.
  4. Apply and verify with the lenderYou submit the admission letter, fee structure and co-applicant details. For larger loans, collateral documents are verified as well.
  5. Sanction and Key Fact StatementYou receive the sanction letter and the Key Fact Statement showing APR, EMI, fees and cooling-off terms. Read them fully before accepting.
  6. Disbursal and after-careThe lender releases funds directly to your bank account. We stay available for questions about repayment, prepayment and your next need.

Fees and charges

  • Processing fee: usually 0% to 1% of the loan, sometimes waived by public sector banks
  • Moratorium interest accrues and may be capitalised
  • Prepayment is often allowed without charge, check lender terms

Aviation and Professional Course Loan: frequently asked questions

Can the loan be disbursed in stages?
Yes, linked to fee installments.
Can I get a loan for any university abroad?
Lenders prefer well-recognised institutions and approved courses. They may fund less than the full cost for unranked ones.
What costs are covered?
Tuition, hostel, travel, books, laptop and other course-related costs, subject to the lender's list.
Can I take a loan after admission?
Yes. In fact, most lenders require the admission letter, so you apply after you have a seat.
