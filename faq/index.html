Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about NEXUS, applying, approval, documents, costs, safety and every loan type.

About NEXUS

What is NEXUS Lending Partners?
NEXUS Lending Partners is a loan facilitation service based in Hyderabad. We help individuals and businesses understand their loan options, prepare documents and connect with regulated partner banks and NBFCs. We do not lend money ourselves.
Is NEXUS a lender?
No. NEXUS does not lend, disburse or collect loan money. All credit decisions are taken by the partner lender. We will name the lender before you share sensitive documents.
How does NEXUS earn money?
Partner lenders may pay NEXUS a commission when a loan is disbursed. We do not charge borrowers advance or processing fees for our assistance. Lenders charge their own fees, which are shown in the Key Fact Statement.
Which loans can NEXUS help with?
Personal, home, loan against property, gold, loan against securities, vehicle, education, business, working capital, trade finance, agriculture, government scheme, instant credit, corporate finance, balance transfers and special-purpose loans.
Where is NEXUS located?
Our office is near Manjeera Trinity Homes, Kukatpally, Hyderabad, Telangana 500072. We also assist customers across India online and on WhatsApp.

Applying and approval

How do I apply through NEXUS?
Choose your loan type, tell us your requirement on the form or WhatsApp, and we will respond with the next steps and a document checklist. The final application is completed with the lender.
How long does approval take?
It depends on the lender, loan type and documents. Simple unsecured loans can be quick, while home, property and business loans take longer. We cannot guarantee any timeline or approval.
Does checking eligibility with NEXUS affect my credit score?
Sharing your details with us does not by itself affect your score. A lender's formal application may trigger a credit enquiry, and we will tell you before that happens.
What if my loan is rejected?
We will help you understand likely reasons and suggest steps or lenders that fit better. Lender decisions are final.
Can I apply with a low credit score?
You may still have options such as secured loans, a co-applicant or lenders with wider criteria. Rates may be higher.

Will you ask for my Aadhaar, PAN or documents on WhatsApp?
For your safety we avoid collecting sensitive ID numbers and OTPs over chat. Documents are shared through the lender's secure channel or a secure upload we will explain at the time.
Do you ask for money before approving a loan?
Never. If anyone asks for an advance fee in NEXUS's name, report it to us at nexuslpteam@gmail.com.
How can I withdraw my consent?

How do I make a complaint?

Costs and terms

What are the typical interest rates?
Rates depend on the loan type, lender, your profile and the market. Pages on this site show indicative ranges only. Always check the APR in the Key Fact Statement.
What documents are needed?
Usually KYC, income proof and bank statements, plus property, vehicle, business or admission documents depending on the loan. Use our document checklist tool.
Can I repay my loan early?
Most loans allow it. Charges depend on the loan type and rate type. Floating-rate loans to individuals carry no prepayment charge under RBI rules.
What is the difference between a bank and an NBFC loan?
Banks are generally cheaper for strong profiles but stricter. NBFCs are often faster and more flexible, sometimes at higher rates. We compare both for you.

Personal Loan FAQs

What is the minimum credit score for a personal loan?
There is no single cut-off. Many lenders prefer 700 or above, and the best rates are usually offered at 750+. Lower scores may still be considered with a higher interest rate or a co-applicant, depending on the lender.
How quickly can I get a personal loan?
Some digital lenders approve within hours when documents are ready and your profile is straightforward. Banks may take 2 to 7 working days. Timelines depend on the lender and verification, and we cannot guarantee a specific disbursal time.
Can I get a personal loan without salary slips?
Self-employed applicants and some salaried borrowers can use bank statements and ITR instead. The options narrow without income proof, so keep 6 to 12 months of bank statements handy.
Will applying hurt my credit score?
A formal application triggers a credit enquiry, which can cause a small, temporary dip. Multiple applications in a short window can add up, so shortlist first.
Is a personal loan better than a credit card EMI?
Often yes for large amounts because personal loan rates are usually lower than credit-card revolving or EMI-conversion rates. Compare the total cost in both cases.
Does NEXUS charge borrowers a fee?
Our assistance is free to borrowers. Partner lenders may pay NEXUS a commission when a loan is disbursed. We will disclose this on request. Lenders charge their own processing fees, which are shown in your Key Fact Statement.

Home Loan FAQs

How much home loan can I get?
Lenders typically fund up to 75% to 90% of the property value depending on the loan amount and income. The actual amount depends on your income, existing EMIs, age, credit score and the lender's valuation of the property.
Which is better: fixed or floating rate?
Floating-rate loans follow market rates and are common for long tenures. Fixed rates give certainty for an initial period and then often reset. Compare the rate-reset terms in the sanction letter.
Can I take a home loan jointly?
Yes. Co-applicants, usually spouse or parents, add their income to eligibility, and co-owners may share tax benefits subject to the law.
What is a pre-approved home loan?
It is an in-principle approval based on your income and credit profile before you choose a property. It helps you negotiate with sellers, but final sanction depends on the property.
What happens if I miss EMIs?
Late payment charges apply and your credit score can drop. Repeated defaults can lead to recovery action under the SARFAESI Act. Talk to your lender early about restructuring if needed.
Does NEXUS help with legal checking of the property?
We guide you on the documents the lender will check. The lender's own legal and technical team verifies the property, and we recommend consulting a property lawyer before paying a token amount.

Loan Against Property (LAP) FAQs

How much can I borrow against my property?
Typically 50% to 70% of the property's market value, depending on property type, location, income and the lender's valuation.
Can I take LAP on a property with an existing home loan?
Usually not on the same property unless the home loan is closed or transferred. A top-up home loan may be a better route.
Is LAP available without ITR?
A few lenders offer low-documentation LAP using bank statements and other proofs, but rates are typically higher. We explain the options after reviewing your profile.
What are the risks?
If you default, the lender can enforce its security interest under the SARFAESI Act. Borrow only what you can service from stable income.
How long does LAP take to disburse?
Legal and technical checks usually take longer than for unsecured loans, often 1 to 3 weeks or more depending on documents and the lender.

Gold Loan FAQs

How much gold loan can I get per gram?
It depends on that day's gold rate, purity and the lender's loan-to-value ratio. Lenders typically lend up to a regulated percentage of the gold's value.
Is my gold safe with the lender?
Reputed banks and NBFCs store gold in secured vaults and insure it. Ask for the lender's custody and insurance terms.
Can I get a gold loan with a low credit score?
Gold loans rely mainly on the collateral, so a low score is often not a barrier, but interest rates may reflect risk.
What happens if I cannot repay?
The lender sends notice and can auction the gold per its policy and law after the due date. Talk to the lender early about extending or renewing.
Which repayment option is best?
Monthly interest lets you repay principal later but is easy to roll. EMI loans reduce principal steadily. Choose based on cash flow.

Loan Against Securities FAQs

Which securities can be pledged?
Lenders maintain approved lists that usually include large-cap shares, selected mutual funds and bonds. Eligibility is lender-specific.
What is a margin call?
If the value of your pledged securities falls below the required level, the lender asks you to add collateral or repay part of the loan. If you do not, the lender can sell securities.
Is it cheaper than a personal loan?
Often yes, because it is secured, but market risk makes it unsuitable for borrowers who cannot absorb a fall.
Can I pledge insurance policies?
Some lenders accept surrender-value policies. Terms vary by policy and insurer.

Vehicle Loan FAQs

What down payment is needed for a car loan?
Often 10% to 20% of the on-road price, though some lenders offer higher loan-to-value for strong profiles.
Can I take a loan for a used car?
Yes, usually with a shorter tenure and slightly higher rate, based on the car's age and valuation.
Are there special loans for electric vehicles?
Many lenders offer EV-specific loans, and some states have subsidies or tax benefits. We check current availability with you.
Can I foreclose a vehicle loan early?
Usually yes, with foreclosure charges depending on your loan terms. Check the clause before signing.

Education Loan FAQs

Is collateral required for an education loan?
Many lenders offer collateral-free loans up to a limit, commonly ₹7.5 lakh, and may require collateral or a guarantor above it. It varies by lender and course.
When do I start repaying?
Typically after a moratorium that covers the course plus 6 to 12 months, but you can pay interest earlier to reduce the burden.
Can I get a loan for any university abroad?
Lenders prefer well-recognised institutions and approved courses. They may fund less than the full cost for unranked ones.
What costs are covered?
Tuition, hostel, travel, books, laptop and other course-related costs, subject to the lender's list.
Can I take a loan after admission?
Yes. In fact, most lenders require the admission letter, so you apply after you have a seat.

Business Loan (MSME) FAQs

What is the minimum business vintage for a loan?
Most lenders prefer at least 1 to 3 years of operation. Startups with strong revenue may be considered by some lenders or through scheme-backed products.
Can I get a business loan without collateral?
Yes, up to certain limits, often with credit-guarantee backing for MSMEs. Larger amounts usually need security.
Do I need a GST registration?
Many lenders require it if you are above the GST threshold. Some products accept bank-statement-based underwriting without GST.
What can I use the loan for?
Working capital, expansion, equipment, inventory, renovation and other business purposes. Personal use or speculation is typically not allowed.
How is a business loan different from a personal loan?
A business loan is assessed on business numbers and used for business needs. A personal loan is assessed on personal income. Mixing them can create tax and compliance problems.

Working Capital & Invoice Finance FAQs

What is the difference between overdraft and cash credit?
Both are revolving limits. Overdraft is often against property or deposits for any business use, while cash credit is typically linked to stock and debtors for working capital.
How does invoice discounting work?
You receive most of the invoice value upfront from a lender or platform and the lender collects from your customer on the due date. You pay a discount charge for the period.
Is GST data used?
Yes. Many lenders use GST returns to assess turnover and allow faster approvals.
What is TReDS?
TReDS (Trade Receivables Discounting System) is an RBI-regulated platform where MSME invoices can be financed by multiple financiers. We can guide you on whether it suits your invoices.

Trade & Export Finance FAQs

What is pre-shipment credit?
A short-term loan to buy raw materials, process and pack goods before shipment against an export order.
What is post-shipment finance?
Finance after goods are shipped, based on shipping documents and invoices, until payment is received.
What is a letter of credit?
A bank's undertaking to pay the seller on presentation of compliant documents. It reduces payment risk in international trade.
Do I need an IEC?
Yes, for import and export of goods, an Importer-Exporter Code from DGFT is generally required.

Agriculture & Rural Loans FAQs

What is the Kisan Credit Card?
KCC is a credit facility for farmers that covers crop cultivation and allied needs. It works as a revolving limit with flexible withdrawals and repayments aligned to harvests.
Is there an interest subsidy?
Eligible farmers can benefit from government interest subvention on short-term crop loans when repaid on time. Rules and rates change, so we check the current notification with you.
Can tenant farmers get loans?
Possible through joint liability groups or with tenancy proofs and local rules, depending on the state and lender.
Are dairy and poultry loans available?
Yes. Many banks and agencies finance animal husbandry, poultry and fisheries with project reports.

Government Scheme Loans FAQs

What are the PM Mudra loan categories?
PMMY loans have four categories: Shishu, Kishor, Tarun and Tarun Plus, covering increasing ticket sizes. Check the latest limits on the official portal.
What is PMEGP?
The Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme is a credit-linked subsidy scheme for setting up new micro-enterprises, implemented through banks and agencies.
What is Stand-Up India?
It facilitates bank loans for SC/ST and women entrepreneurs to set up greenfield enterprises in manufacturing, services, trading or allied activities.
What is PM SVANidhi?
A scheme providing working-capital loans to street vendors, with incentives for regular repayment and digital transactions.
Is there an agent fee for government loans?
Official applications do not require a fee to anyone. Be careful about anyone asking for advance payment to 'guarantee' approval.

Instant & Small-Ticket Credit FAQs

What is BNPL?
Buy Now, Pay Later lets you purchase now and pay later in one or more instalments. Late payment fees can make it costly, and it can affect your credit score.
What is earned wage access?
It lets employees draw part of already-earned salary before payday, usually for a small fee, through employer-linked platforms.
How do I avoid loan-app fraud?
Use only apps linked to RBI-regulated lenders listed in RBI's public directory of digital lending apps. Never grant contact or gallery access to unknown apps, and never pay an advance fee.
Are P2P loans safe?
P2P platforms are regulated as NBFC-P2P and returns or repayments depend on borrower repayment. They carry risk.

Corporate & Project Finance FAQs

What is project finance?
Funding for a specific project, repaid from the project's own cash flows, with project assets as security.
What is venture debt?
Debt for venture-backed startups, usually with warrants or fees, offering growth capital without immediate equity dilution.
What is mezzanine financing?
A hybrid between debt and equity that ranks below senior debt and carries higher returns for the lender.
How long do these transactions take?
Typically several weeks to several months depending on size, diligence and approvals.

Debt Consolidation & Balance Transfer FAQs

When is a balance transfer worthwhile?
When the rate difference is meaningful, enough tenure remains and total fees are low. Use our balance transfer calculator.
Will my credit score drop?
A new loan application triggers an enquiry, and closing old loans can change your credit mix. Timely repayment later usually improves the score.
Can I consolidate credit card dues?
Yes, using a personal loan, usually at lower rates than revolving credit card interest, provided you stop using the card for new revolving debt.
What is loan restructuring?
Renegotiating the tenure or EMI with your lender when you face difficulty, which may affect your credit report.

Lifestyle & Special-Purpose Loans FAQs

Are medical loans available for elective procedures?
Many lenders finance planned treatments such as dental, cosmetic or fertility care, subject to their policies.
Is a rental deposit loan possible?
Some lenders and apps offer rental deposit financing, which is typically a short personal loan.
Is there a subsidy for solar?
Government subsidy programs for rooftop solar exist, subject to eligibility and current rules. We check the latest conditions for you.
