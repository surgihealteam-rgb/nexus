How it works

From question to disbursal in four clear steps

You stay in control at every step. The lender takes the final decision and pays you directly.

Frequently asked questions

What is NEXUS Lending Partners?
NEXUS Lending Partners is a loan facilitation service based in Hyderabad. We help individuals and businesses understand their loan options, prepare documents and connect with regulated partner banks and NBFCs. We do not lend money ourselves.
Is NEXUS a lender?
No. NEXUS does not lend, disburse or collect loan money. All credit decisions are taken by the partner lender. We will name the lender before you share sensitive documents.
How does NEXUS earn money?
Partner lenders may pay NEXUS a commission when a loan is disbursed. We do not charge borrowers advance or processing fees for our assistance. Lenders charge their own fees, which are shown in the Key Fact Statement.
How do I apply through NEXUS?
Choose your loan type, tell us your requirement on the form or WhatsApp, and we will respond with the next steps and a document checklist. The final application is completed with the lender.
Is my data safe?
We collect only what is needed, with your consent, and share it only with lenders you agree to. We do not ask for OTPs, passwords or card PINs. See our Privacy Policy.
What are the typical interest rates?
Rates depend on the loan type, lender, your profile and the market. Pages on this site show indicative ranges only. Always check the APR in the Key Fact Statement.
Can I apply with a low credit score?
You may still have options such as secured loans, a co-applicant or lenders with wider criteria. Rates may be higher.
